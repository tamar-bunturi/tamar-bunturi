## Professional Approach

While ensuring a smooth and intuitive user experience is important, I also focus on the underlying business logic, system behavior, data integrity, and workflows that drive the product. Many critical defects are not visible on the surface and can directly impact business operations, customer trust, and revenue.

My approach is to evaluate software from both a user perspective and a business perspective, identifying risks that could affect functionality, integrations, processes, or overall product goals.
